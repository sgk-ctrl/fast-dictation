# Fast Dictate Design System

## Scene

A builder is alone at a desk late in the evening, speaking a rough thought into a MacBook Air while a notes app waits in the foreground. The interface should be calm enough for long thinking, but tactile enough to feel like a recording instrument.

## Color Strategy

Committed warm amber on deep graphite. Amber signals live capture without looking like an alarm. Graphite keeps the page quiet and Mac-native.

## Palette

- `--ink`: `oklch(17% 0.014 74)`
- `--panel`: `oklch(22% 0.018 74)`
- `--paper`: `oklch(93% 0.018 84)`
- `--muted`: `oklch(76% 0.025 78)`
- `--amber`: `oklch(76% 0.17 72)`
- `--copper`: `oklch(58% 0.13 46)`
- `--green`: `oklch(72% 0.11 142)`

## Typography

Use the system font stack for product trust and speed. The landing page can use heavy system display sizing, tight letter spacing, and tabular numerals for timer-like details.

## Shape Language

- Rounded rectangles should feel like Mac hardware, not bubbly SaaS cards.
- Use fine 1px lines, recessed panels, and waveform geometry.
- Prefer instrument panels, meters, and timelines over repeated feature cards.

## Motion

Keep motion short and stateful. Use pulsing only for recording state. Avoid decorative animation.
