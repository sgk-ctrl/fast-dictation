# Fast Dictate Product Context

## Register

brand

## Product Purpose

Fast Dictate is a local-first macOS dictation app for people who think better out loud and want their words to appear in any app without sending audio to a cloud service.

## Users

- Builders who write issues, notes, prompts, specs, and messages faster by speaking.
- Privacy-sensitive users who do not want raw voice notes uploaded to a third-party service.
- Mac power users who want a menu bar utility that disappears until needed.

## Brand Voice

Warm, private, mechanical. It should feel like a pocket field recorder connected directly to your cursor.

## Strategic Principles

- Local by default: audio and transcription stay on the Mac.
- Low ceremony: one menu bar click starts capture.
- Long thought support: voice notes should handle minutes, not just short commands.
- Plain outputs: copied text should be usable immediately in notes, chat, docs, and code tools.

## Anti-References

- Cloud AI dashboards with generic gradient blobs.
- Meeting transcription tools that imply surveillance or team analytics.
- Heavy editors that force users to manage recordings before getting text.
